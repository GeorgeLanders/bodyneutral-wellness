# Play Store Readiness

The app is a promising prototype moving toward MVP. It is not fully launch-ready yet, but the core is strong.

## Current Technical Status

Passing checks:

- Unit tests.
- Android test compilation.
- Android lint.
- Debug APK build.
- Connected Android UI test on physical Samsung device.
- AI proxy syntax check.

## Positioning

Recommended store positioning:

```text
Shame-free wellness support for all bodies: gentle movement, mindful nourishment, stress support, journaling, and sustainable habit-based weight management.
```

## Target Audience

- Beginners who dislike aggressive fitness apps.
- Men and women seeking sustainable wellness.
- People with body-confidence goals.
- People with weight-management goals who want shame-free support.
- People looking for gentle movement and stress tools.
- Plus-size users and users with mobility needs.

## Before Closed Testing

Complete:

- Visual QA on all screens.
- Copy QA for tone and text clipping.
- Confirm all videos play correctly.
- Confirm onboarding reset works.
- Confirm erase all data works.
- Confirm recommendation feedback updates.
- Confirm AI proxy fallback works.
- Add privacy policy draft.
- Add wellness/medical/crisis disclaimers.
- Add app screenshots.
- Add app icon and feature graphic review.

## Closed Testing Plan

Recruit 20 to 50 testers:

- Men.
- Women.
- Plus-size users.
- Beginners.
- Users with weight-management goals.
- Users seeking stress support.
- Users with low-impact movement needs.

Ask testers:

- Did the app feel emotionally safe?
- Did the app feel useful daily?
- What screen confused you?
- What would make you come back tomorrow?
- Did any copy feel shame-based or exclusionary?
- Would you recommend it?

## Suggested Store Copy

Short description:

```text
Shame-free wellness, gentle movement, journaling, and habit support for all bodies.
```

Full description draft:

```text
BodyNeutral Wellness is a calmer daily wellness companion for people who want sustainable habits without shame. Check in with your mood, choose gentle movement, reflect in a body-neutral journal, track nourishment and hydration, use SOS grounding tools, and get personalized recommendations for your next supportive step.

Whether your goals are energy, confidence, mobility, stress relief, mindful nourishment, strength, or supportive weight management, the app helps you build habits you can live with.

This app provides general wellness support only. It is not medical advice, mental health treatment, crisis care, or eating-disorder treatment.
```

## Launch Risks

- Wellness apps need trust. Weak privacy or vague disclaimers can hurt launch readiness.
- Weight-management language must avoid guarantees and shame.
- AI features need clear disclosure.
- Backend proxy needs deployment, rate limits, HTTPS, and minimal logging.
- Store screenshots must look polished and consistent.

## MVP Launch Criteria

Ready for public MVP when:

- Closed testing feedback is mostly positive.
- No major crashes.
- All store policy requirements are satisfied.
- Privacy policy is published.
- AI backend is secure or AI is disabled for launch.
- Screenshots and app listing are polished.
