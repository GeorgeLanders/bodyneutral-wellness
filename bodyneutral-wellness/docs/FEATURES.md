# Features

## Positioning

BodyNeutral Wellness is a shame-free wellness companion for all bodies. It supports gentle movement, mindful nourishment, daily reflection, stress support, body confidence, sustainable habits, and optional weight-management goals.

## Dashboard

- Personalized greeting based on time of day, mood, Gentle Day Mode, and streak.
- Daily mood check-in.
- Gentle Day Mode for tender body-image or low-energy days.
- 7-day check-in pattern.
- Wellness wins card with streak badge.
- Daily affirmation or custom affirmation.
- Recommended for You cards.
- What should I do now? button.
- Weekly wellness reflection.
- Daily intention selector.
- Gentle habits checklist.
- Community, gear guide, and quick action links.
- Floating SOS button.

## Recommendation Engine

Recommendations are generated locally from:

- Gentle Day Mode.
- Mood check-in.
- Streak count.
- Hydration cups.
- Movement minutes.
- Nourishment count.
- Sleep hours.
- Journal entry count.
- Habit completion.
- Daily intention.
- Local recommendation feedback.

Destinations include:

- Breathing.
- Journal.
- Movement.
- Nourish.
- SOS.
- Wellness Wins.

Feedback:

- Helpful.
- Not today.

The engine favors helpful recommendation types and lowers repeatedly dismissed types.

## AI Wellness Coach

The coach supports:

- Offline wellness responses.
- Optional proxy-backed AI responses.
- Text-to-speech playback.
- Quick support prompts.
- Smart SOS action chips when distress is detected.
- Local coach memory for recent themes such as stress, rest, nourishment, movement, body image, and weight management.

## SOS Grounding

The SOS screen includes:

- Trigger categories: General, Body Image, Overwhelm, Food/Eating, Sleepless.
- Rotating grounding cards.
- Mini breathing anchor.
- 5-4-3-2-1 sensory grounding flow.
- Quick links to breathing and coach.

## Journal

- Context-aware AI-style prompt generator.
- Prompt refresh option.
- Local journal entries.
- Body-neutral reflection framing.

## Nourish

- Mindful savoring check-in.
- Taste, texture, smell, satisfaction, and note logging.
- Hydration tracker.
- Body-neutral meal ideas.
- Nourish insights based on logs and hydration.
- No diet judgment or moral food scoring.

## Movement

- Gentle movement video library.
- Categories: seated, low impact, joyful.
- Custom movement flow builder.
- Guided flow player with timer.
- Ambient sound options.
- Completion reflection.

## Tracker

- Gentle self-care habits.
- Hydration.
- Sleep.
- Movement.
- Optional energy awareness for fuel, performance, or weight-management goals.
- Custom habit creation.

## Profile

- Name and custom affirmation.
- Streak badge.
- Daily intention display.
- Gentle achievement badges.
- Wellness goals summary.
- Movement preference.
