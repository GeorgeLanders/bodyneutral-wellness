# Privacy And Safety

BodyNeutral Wellness handles sensitive wellness-adjacent data. The app should be positioned carefully and honestly.

## Current Data

The Android app stores data locally in SharedPreferences, including:

- Name.
- Onboarding goals.
- Mobility preference.
- Custom affirmation.
- Daily tracker values.
- Hydration.
- Movement minutes.
- Sleep hours.
- Optional energy awareness.
- Journal entries.
- Community posts.
- Coach memory themes.
- Recommendation feedback.
- Daily check-ins.
- Nourish logs.
- Settings.
- AI proxy URL.

## Sensitive Areas

The app may include:

- Journal text.
- Body-image distress.
- Stress and anxiety text.
- Food and nourishment notes.
- Weight-management goals.
- Voice/audio diary functionality.
- AI coach messages if proxy is enabled.

## Required Disclaimers

Before Play Store release, include clear disclaimers:

- The app provides general wellness support.
- The app is not medical advice.
- The app is not mental health treatment.
- The app is not crisis care.
- The app is not eating-disorder treatment.
- Weight-management content is general habit support and does not guarantee results.
- Users should contact a qualified professional for medical, nutrition, mental health, or emergency needs.

## AI Disclosure

If the AI proxy is enabled, disclose:

- Messages may be sent to a server to generate AI responses.
- AI responses can be wrong or incomplete.
- Users should not rely on AI for medical, crisis, or emergency decisions.
- API keys are not stored in the Android app.

## Privacy Policy Needs

Before Play Store release, create a public privacy policy URL explaining:

- What data is collected.
- What data is stored locally.
- What data may be sent to the AI proxy.
- Whether data is logged by the proxy.
- How users can delete data.
- Whether data is shared with third parties.
- Contact information.

## Data Safety Form Notes

Google Play Data Safety will likely need answers for:

- Health and fitness data.
- Personal info if name is collected.
- Audio if audio diary records or processes voice.
- App activity.
- User-generated content.
- Data deletion support.
- Data encryption in transit if proxy is used.

## Recommended Safety Improvements

- Add in-app Privacy and Safety screen.
- Add first-run consent for AI proxy use.
- Add crisis resources screen.
- Add local export/delete controls for journal and nourish logs.
- Add optional PIN or biometric lock for journal.
- Add backend rate limiting and minimal logging.
